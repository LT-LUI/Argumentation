### 2. Decompose the actual dispute

| Question | Possible disagreement |
|---|---|
| **What counts as “Islam”?** | Qur'an? Hadith? Muhammad's conduct? classical jurisprudence? historical Muslim societies? beliefs of Muslims today? |
| **What does “of peace” mean?** | Principally advocates peace? prohibits aggressive violence? more peaceful than alternatives? predominantly interpreted peacefully? |
| **Who determines authentic Islam?** | Text? traditional scholars? historical consensus? individual believers? contemporary Muslim majority? |
| **Can contradictory interpretations both be authentically Islamic?** | Central question |
| **Does violent scripture make a religion non-peaceful?** | Or does actual interpretation matter more? |
| **Does peaceful behaviour by most believers establish anything about the doctrine?** | Sociological versus theological claim |
| **Why does Islamist violence arise?** | Theology, politics, identity, grievance, institutions, combinations? |
| **How relevant is Muhammad's historical conduct?** | Foundational precedent versus historically contextual behaviour |

### Analysis of the transcript
- **Clean speaker identities + debate structure**
- **Extract atomic claims/arguments**, with IDs such as `K1`, `N3`, `HA5`, `DM2`
- **Separate main arguments from illustrations, rhetoric, anecdotes and factual evidence**
- **Standardise the important arguments** into premises/conclusions
- **Build an argument map**, including attacks on premises, warrants and conclusions
- **Apply Toulmin / argumentation schemes** selectively to the interesting cases
- **Identify the real underlying disputes**, especially definitions of _Islam_, _peace_, _authentic interpretation_, and causation
- **Evaluate the strongest arguments on both sides and their rebuttals**
- **Reconstruct a much better version of the debate**
- Only then form a substantive conclusion.
