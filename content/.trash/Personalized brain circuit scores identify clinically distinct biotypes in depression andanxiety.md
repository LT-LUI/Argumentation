- **Default Mode Network (DMN):**
	- **Function:** Governs self-referential thought, mind-wandering, and rumination.
	- **Relevance:** Dysfunction in this circuit is linked to excessive rumination and negative self-focus, common in depression.
- **Salience Network (SN):**
	- **Function:** Detects and responds to emotionally and behaviorally significant stimuli.
	- **Relevance:** Impairments in the SN can lead to difficulty identifying and prioritizing important stimuli, contributing to emotional dysregulation in anxiety and depression.
- **Frontoparietal Network (FPN):**
	- **Function:** Supports cognitive control, attention, and goal-directed behavior.
	- **Relevance:** Dysfunction here can result in impaired decision-making and difficulty regulating thoughts and actions.
- **Limbic Circuits:**
	- **Function:** Includes regions like the amygdala, which process emotions and threats.
	- **Relevance:** Hyperactivity in limbic circuits is commonly associated with heightened anxiety and fear responses.
